### 👨‍💻 About My Forge

I specialize in bridging the gap between robust architecture, striking front-end design, and cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence. Operating out of a Linux environment, my focus is developing complete, intelligent ecosystems.

* 🎨 **Design Philosophy:** High-contrast, premium visual identities (Dark & Gold is my signature aesthetic) with intuitive UX.
* 🤖 **AI Integration:** Crafting smart applications utilizing **LLMs**, building **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** pipelines, **Model Fine-Tuning**, and applying advanced **Prompt Engineering** to deliver context-aware user experiences.
* ⚙️ **Engineering Focus:** Seamless front-end interfaces, robust authentication pipelines, and containerized architectures.
